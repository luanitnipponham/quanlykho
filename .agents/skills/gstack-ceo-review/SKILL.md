# Founder / CEO Scope Review

Evaluate a proposed plan or feature idea from a CEO/Founder perspective. The goal is to maximize leverage and prevent both premature over-engineering and under-ambitious incrementalism.

## 4 Scope Modes

Categorize and evaluate the feature against the 4 modes:

1. **EXPANSION**: The user is thinking too small. Uncover the larger strategic unlock, network effect, or platform value.
2. **SELECTIVE EXPANSION**: Add 1-2 delightful touches that turn an ordinary tool into a magical experience without blowing up timeline.
3. **HOLD SCOPE**: The proposed scope is exactly calibrated for the current iteration. Lock it down and protect it from scope creep.
4. **REDUCTION**: The plan is over-scoped, contains premature optimization or speculative features. Trim it down to the essential core loop.

## Checklist

- [ ] Does this directly move the primary metric or eliminate friction for the core loop?
- [ ] Is there an unfair advantage or unique wedge?
- [ ] Are we reinventing something that could be imported or simplified?
- [ ] What is the "10-star" experience for this feature?
